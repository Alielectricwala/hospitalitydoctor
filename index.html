    FAQ

    Questions hoteliers ask.

    What does The Hospitality Doctor do?

    We audit your hotel's people, processes and profit, then fix the gaps on the floor with workshops, SOPs, upselling and revenue programmes. Every module comes with 30 days of reinforcement.

    Who is Sanchita Ralhan?

    She's the founder. She has 15+ years in luxury hospitality with The Leela, Oberoi, Shangri-La, Conrad, Indian Accent and Samode, has run her own hotel, and now consults for hotels across India from Jaipur.

    How is this different from regular hotel training?

    There's no generic calendar. Every engagement starts with a diagnosis, and every module comes with a hands-on workshop, a playbook your team keeps, and 30 days of follow-through.

    What is the 48-hour diagnostic?

    We spend 48 hours inside your hotel watching shifts, guest touchpoints, housekeeping, F&B service and pricing. Then we show you what we'd fix first.

    Which hotels do you work with?

    Independent, boutique, heritage and resort hotels, wedding venues and hotel portfolios. Recent work includes Samode, Arya Niwas, City Palace Bikaner, Blackrock, Cinnamon Hotels and Sangam Farms.

    Do you work outside Rajasthan?

    Yes. We're based in Jaipur and work on-property with hotels across India.

    Can you help with a new hotel opening?

    Yes. Pre-Opening Support covers SOPs, job descriptions, training plans, mock service runs and readiness checks before the first guest arrives.
