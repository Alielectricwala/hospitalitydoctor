Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: we only collect what you send us or what you allow us to measure. We use it to reply to you and to improve this website. We never sell your data.

Who we are

This website is run by SRK Hospitality Solutions, trading as The Hospitality Doctor, based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, email sanchita@srkhospitality.net.

What we collect

  • Enquiries you send us: your name, hotel or property, email, optional phone number and your message, submitted through our booking form.
  • Conversations you start: details you share when you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Usage analytics, only if you allow it: aggregated page views, referrers, device type, country and page-speed metrics, collected through Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights.
  • Security logs: our hosting provider briefly processes IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a diagnosis or proposal.
  • To understand how the site is used and keep it fast, if you consent.
  • To prevent spam and abuse of our forms.

We process enquiry data with your consent and to take steps you ask for before any engagement. This is consistent with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where relevant, the GDPR.

Cookies and analytics

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. If you choose Allow in our privacy banner, we load Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. These are cookieless and collect only aggregated, anonymised data. We save your choice in your browser's local storage under thd-consent-v1 so we don't ask again. You can change it at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

Who we share it with

  • Vercel Inc.: website hosting and, if you consent, analytics.
  • Resend: delivering form enquiries to our inbox.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): only if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy terms. Some may process it outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client, in which case we keep them for as long as the law requires. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance by emailing sanchita@srkhospitality.net. We'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
